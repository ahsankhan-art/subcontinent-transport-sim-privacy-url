Privacy Policy

Subcontinent Transport Simulator 3D · Last updated: 29 September 2026

This policy explains what the mobile game Subcontinent Transport Simulator 3D ("the game", "we") collects, why, and what choices you have. It applies to the Android and iOS versions of the game.

The short version. The game works without an account and we never ask for your name, address or phone number. We collect anonymous gameplay statistics and crash reports only after you agree, and the game shows ads, including skippable video ads. Your progress is saved on your own device.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

None is required. The game has no sign-up, no login and no profile. If you contact us by e-mail, we see the address you write from and whatever you put in the message.

Information collected automatically

WhatWhyWhen
Gameplay events: levels started, finished or failed, stars earned, vehicles used, distance drivenTo see which levels are too hard or too easy and improve the gameOnly after you accept analytics

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data. The game does not ask for those permissions.

2. Advertising, including skippable video ads

The game is free and supported by advertising. It shows:

  • Skippable video ads — short video ads you can skip after a few seconds, shown between missions.
  • Rewarded video ads — optional ads you choose to watch in exchange for a reward, such as doubling your coins or a free repair. You never have to watch one to keep playing.
  • Banner and interstitial ads — shown between missions, never while you are driving.

Our advertising partner may use your device's advertising identifier to limit repeats, measure whether an ad worked, and (if you allow it) choose ads based on your interests. You can turn off personalised ads at any time in Settings → Privacy inside the game, and you can reset or delete the advertising identifier in your device settings. On iOS, the game only requests app tracking permission for personalised ads, and declining changes nothing else about how the game works. Turning personalised ads off does not remove ads; it makes them less relevant.

Ads are delivered by Google AdMob. Google's own privacy policy explains how it handles data: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

3. Purchases

Optional purchases (gem packs, bundles, removing ads) are handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see or store your card number or billing address. We receive only a confirmation that a purchase succeeded, so the game can unlock the item.

4. Where your data is stored

Your progress — levels, stars, coins, vehicles and settings — is saved in a file on your own device. Deleting the game deletes it. We do not upload your save to a server, and there is no cloud account.

5. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 (under 16 in the European Economic Area), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, e-mail us and we will delete it. Where the app store rates the game for a family audience, only non-personalised ads are requested.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Analytics and crash reports: off until you accept, and you can change it at any time in Settings → Privacy.
  • Personalised ads: switch off in Settings → Privacy, or reset your advertising identifier in your device settings.
  • Deleting your data: uninstalling the game removes everything stored on your device. To ask us to delete analytics or crash data already collected, e-mail us with your device model and approximate dates.
  • If you are in the EEA or UK (GDPR): you may ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of your data, and may object to processing. Our legal basis is your consent for analytics and ads, and legitimate interest for keeping the game secure and working.
  • If you are in California (CCPA/CPRA): you may ask what categories of data were collected and request deletion. We do not sell personal information.

7. Data retention

Anonymous analytics and crash data are kept for up to 14 months and then deleted or aggregated. Data on your device stays until you delete the game.

8. Security

Data sent to our analytics and advertising partners travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). No method of transmission is perfectly secure, but we do not store personal data on our own servers.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes and the new version appears on this page. Significant changes will also be announced inside the game.
